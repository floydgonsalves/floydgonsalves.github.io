floyd-nas privacy policy

"floyd-nas" is a private tool used only by its owner, Floyd Gonsalves, and his household. It is not offered to anyone else.

It reads the owner's own Gmail accounts to find messages, and creates draft replies that the owner reviews and sends himself. It never sends mail. It adds events to the household's own Google Calendar.

The data stays on the owner's own computer and storage. It is not collected, shared or sold, and no one else's account is accessed.

Google user data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
